## Free Exercise DB

- Project: https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db
- Pinned revision: f00c92c7dcf1216a928a52c3706c7ce8e2f71ed5
- Data: https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/blob/f00c92c7dcf1216a928a52c3706c7ce8e2f71ed5/dist/exercises.json
- License: Unlicense, verified 2026-10-12.
- LiftLog selects 140 repetition-oriented exercises from 876 source entries. English names, primary/secondary muscle groups, and difficulty are derived from the source. Stable source IDs are retained in docs/exercise-library.js.
- Chinese names and aliases, broader body-part categories, full-body tags, and normalized equipment labels are local editorial adaptations. Smith machines, pull-up/dip bars, and suspension straps are separated from the upstream broad machine/body-only/other categories. Equipment identifies the principal implement, not every bench or rack required.
- No source photographs, image URLs, scripts, or detailed exercise instructions are bundled. Catalog membership is not a safety endorsement or an individualized training recommendation. The database includes some legacy instructional claims; these instructions are deliberately not reproduced.
- Existing default exercise names remain unchanged to preserve exact-name training-history lookup. Data schema stays v2; selecting a library item copies its editable Chinese name into a plan/session and does not replace existing records.
